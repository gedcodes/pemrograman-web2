## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a balanced hybrid of modern tactile neumorphism and high-density retail administrative utility. Rooted in an organic Indonesian botanical-teal aesthetic, it projects quiet confidence, mechanical precision, and tactile warmth. The interface serves store managers, retail cashiers, and inventory operators who require high data throughput without visual fatigue during long terminal shifts.

### Design Style: Modern Neumorphism ("Soft Chrome, Crisp Data")
Traditional neumorphism often compromises accessibility through washed-out contrast. This design system resolves that failure through a bifurcated architectural rule:
1. **Soft for Chrome**: Structural canvases, macro containers, filter chips, and primary hardware-like controls use dual-directional ambient light modeling on an organic `#E4EDEA` sage-teal canvas.
2. **Crisp for Data**: High-density zones—including operational data tables, checkout receipts, financial line items, and modal dialogs—transition instantly into crisp `#FFFFFF` surfaces with explicit 1px `#CBDAD5` borders.
3. **No Decorative Excess**: Gradients, decorative neon glows, drop-shadow blurs on SVG paths, and emojis are strictly forbidden. The tactile sensation originates solely from physical top-left lighting, directional inset presses, and calibrated border radii.

## Colors

The system uses an intentional botanical-mineral color architecture that avoids harsh black-and-white polarities in favor of chromatic stability.

### Canvas & Surfaces
- The default canvas is `--surface-base` (`#E4EDEA`), a muted, low-strain teal-tinted gray that forms the base plane for top-left highlights and shadow extrusion.
- `--surface-raised` (`#EDF4F2`) is deployed on floating structural panels like the POS right-hand cart rail.
- `--surface-crisp` (`#FFFFFF`) serves as the designated canvas for financial readouts, data tables, modals, and physical receipt emulations.
- `--surface-sunken` (`#D7E4E0`) is used for pressed button states, input wells, and thumbnail bays.

### Brand & Interactive Colors
- **Primary Teal** (`#0B6B62`): The active workhorse for primary affirmative CTAs, active filters, and interactive selections.
- **Deep Teal** (`#0B2B27`): A saturated slab container that anchors the full-height navigation sidebar, providing an immediate visual anchor with a >12:1 contrast ratio against the light canvas.
- **Transactional Green** (`#1B7A43`): Reserved exclusively for irreversible POS transactions ("BAYAR", "Konfirmasi Lunas", "Payment Received").
- **Destructive Crimson** (`#B23A16`): Deployed for voiding transactions, deleting catalog items, or revoking access.

### Contrast & Hierarchy Rules
1. Never place low-contrast text on soft surfaces. All primary headings and body values use `--text-primary` (`#16302C`), passing WCAG AAA standards against the `#E4EDEA` canvas.
2. Status badges must use the paired pill tokens (e.g., `--success-text` `#12694A` over `--success-bg` `#CFE7DB`). Do not invent standalone border outlines or uncontained color text for statuses.

## Typography

The typography strategy leverages strict functional role segregation across three typefaces:

1. **Outfit (Display & Headings)**: Geometric yet warm, used strictly for top-level navigation titles, page headers, metric summaries, and modal titles. Page headers (`headline-lg`) are hard-capped at 26px to retain admin compactness.
2. **Plus Jakarta Sans (Operational Body & Controls)**: Contemporary, humanist, and highly legible at micro-sizes. Used for descriptive text, form field labels, list rows, and tooltips.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Financial & Tabular Ledger Data)**: Monospaced glyphs applied to all inventory counts, barcodes, invoice IDs, and Indonesian Rupiah currency values.

### Currency Formatting
- Financial amounts must be formatted with the prefix `Rp ` (e.g., `Rp 4.317.500`).
- The `Rp ` prefix uses `--text-muted` scaled down to `0.8em`, while the numeric digits retain full opacity and standard monospaced weighting.
- Use periods (`.`) as thousands separators. Decimals are suppressed for whole IDR amounts.
- All numeric ledger rows must be right-aligned within data table cells.

## Layout & Spacing

The interface uses an 8px base rhythm (with a 4px sub-grid for badges and field labels).

### Spatial Grid & Screen Divisions
- **Desktop Layout (≥1280px)**: Three-column split: Fixed 260px dark sidebar (`#0B2B27`), fluid operational workspace (12-column sub-grid, 20px gutters, max-width 1440px), and an optional sticky 420px checkout cart panel spanning full viewport height (`100dvh`).
- **Compact Desktop (1024px – 1279px)**: Sidebar automatically collapses to a 72px icon-only rail. The main workspace expands to consume freed real estate.
- **Tablet & Mobile (<1024px)**: The sidebar converts to an off-canvas drawer. Multi-column grids (`2fr 1fr 1fr` or `70/30` splits) drop into a single vertical stack (`1fr`). The POS checkout panel detaches into an expandable bottom drawer.

### Interior Padding Guardrails
- **Card Padding**: Neumorphic soft cards must never have less than 16px of inner padding (standard is 24px). Lower padding values cause the negative top-left light highlight to clip against child bounding boxes.
- **Table Cell Padding**: Standardized at 14px vertical and 16px horizontal to ensure dense, scan-efficient ledger inspection.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through calibrated top-left directional illumination. A virtual light angle cast from 315° creates soft, natural extruded surfaces over the `#E4EDEA` canvas.

### Neumorphic Shadow Tokens
1. **Raised Sm** (Chips, pagination pills, toggles):
   `4px 4px 10px rgba(122, 152, 145, 0.30), -4px -4px 10px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.90)`
2. **Raised Md** (Canvas cards, product tiles, secondary tactile controls):
   `10px 10px 24px rgba(122, 152, 145, 0.32), -8px -8px 20px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`
3. **Raised Lg** (Modals, checkout sheets, floating flyouts):
   `24px 24px 60px rgba(90, 120, 113, 0.28), -16px -16px 40px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.90)`
4. **Sunken / Inset** (Form text inputs, active pressed states, well containers):
   `inset 4px 4px 10px rgba(122, 152, 145, 0.38), inset -3px -3px 8px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`

### Crisp Surface Elevation
Tables, receipts, and toast overlays do not use dual soft shadows. Instead, they sit flat on `#FFFFFF` with a crisp composite edge:
`0 18px 40px -18px rgba(22, 48, 44, 0.14), 0 0 0 1px rgba(203, 218, 213, 0.80)`

### Interaction Rules
- **Tactile Depress**: On `:active`, clickable raised elements swap from `Raised` shadows to `Sunken` shadows and scale down to `0.985` over 140ms.
- **Focus Rings**: Form inputs break out of the soft system with an explicit outer ring: `0 0 0 1.5px #0D7C74, 0 0 0 5px rgba(13, 124, 116, 0.24)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a balanced geometry characterized by sweeping container radiuses and ergonomically tailored touch points.

### Radius System
- **Pill (`999px`)**: Status badges, filter tags, category pills, avatar chips, and quick-add increment counters.
- **`28px`**: Modal popups and confirmation dialog sheets.
- **`24px`**: Structural panels, bento metric cards, and `#FFFFFF` data table containers.
- **`20px`**: POS product grid tiles and media upload containers.
- **`14px`**: Action buttons, text input fields, dropdown trigger buttons, and search inputs.
- **`12px`**: Interactive navigation links in the sidebar.
- **`8px`**: Data cell thumbnails, image previews, and skeleton loader blocks.

### Borders
Borders must remain minimal. Use `1px solid #CBDAD5` for structural separators and table rows. Never apply dark borders to neumorphic cards; edge boundaries are defined solely by the opposing white-and-teal shadow geometry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action ("Simpan", "Tambah")**: 48px height, 14px radius, filled with `#0B6B62`, text `#FFFFFF`. Shadow: `0 6px 16px -6px rgba(11, 107, 98, 0.55)`. Hover: `#09514A` with a -1px translation.
- **Transactional CTA ("BAYAR")**: Full-width 56px height, 14px radius, filled with `#1B7A43`, text `#FFFFFF`. Font: Outfit 16px 600 weight.
- **Soft / Secondary**: 48px height, 14px radius, canvas-colored surface (`#E4EDEA`) with `Raised Sm` dual shadow and `#16302C` text. Pressing activates the `Sunken` shadow.
- **Destructive**: 48px height, 14px radius, solid `#B23A16` with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Form Inputs & Barcode Bars
- **Height**: 48px standard input, 56px for the primary barcode/global search bar.
- **Styling**: `Sunken` inset dual shadow over canvas, 14px border radius, 0 16px inner padding.
- **Labels**: 13.5px Plus Jakarta Sans (600 weight), positioned 8px directly above the control.
- **Error State**: Inset shadow persists, overlaid with an inner border of `1.5px solid #B23A16`, accompanied by an inline 12px error caption.

### Data Tables
- **Container**: Elevated `#FFFFFF` crisp card, 24px radius, 1px border (`#CBDAD5`).
- **Header**: Sticky `#F4F8F7` bar, 11px uppercase overline text (`#47625D`), letter spacing `0.08em`.
- **Row Styling**: Fixed 52px height, separated by 1px solid `#CBDAD5`. No individual shadows.
- **Row Hover**: Smooth transition to `rgba(11, 107, 98, 0.05)`.
- **Numeric Columns**: Rendered in `JetBrains Mono` 13.5px, right-aligned.

### Product POS Grid Cards
- **Dimensions**: ~180px × 200px, 20px radius, styled with `Raised Md` neumorphic shadow.
- **Contents**: 72px × 72px thumbnail set in a `#D7E4E0` sunken recess, followed by a 13.5px product title and a bold right-aligned `JetBrains Mono` price.
- **Active Selection**: Smooth shift to `Sunken` inset shadow, indicating item insertion into the cart.

### Status Chips & Badges
- **Shape**: Full pill (`999px` radius), padding `4px 12px`.
- **Palette Rules**:
  - *Success / In Stock*: `#12694A` text on `#CFE7DB` fill.
  - *Warning / Low Stock*: `#6F5000` text on `#F7E6B5` fill.
  - *Danger / Out of Stock*: `#9E3210` text on `#F6DACF` fill.
  - *Info / Draft*: `#0B6158` text on `#D0E7E3` fill.

### Receipts & Modals
- **Receipt Container**: Fixed 320px width `#FFFFFF` card, flat crisp border, finished with a classic CSS zig-zag saw-tooth bottom edge.
- **Modal Backdrops**: `rgba(11, 43, 39, 0.45)` fill paired with a `backdrop-filter: blur(6px)`.
